## Further Reading

- Raber, M. S. 2002. "[Survey and Inventory of the Hampshire and Hampden Canal (New Haven and Northampton Canal)](https://docs.farmingtoncanal.info/HampshireHampdenCanal-Raber2002.pdf)."
- Camposeo, J. M. 1977. "[The History of the Canal System between New Haven and Northampton (1822-1847)](https://docs.farmingtoncanal.info/Camposeo_Canal_History_1977.pdf)." *Historical Journal of Western Massachusetts*.
- McMahon, F. B. 1950s. "[Canal Traffic](https://docs.farmingtoncanal.info/McMahon_Canal_Traffic.pdf)."
- Connecticut WPA Writers' Project. 1941. *[Boats Across New England Hills: The Story of the Farmington Canal](https://docs.farmingtoncanal.info/WPA_Farmington_Canal_1941.pdf)*.
- Harte, C. R. 1938. *Connecticut's Canals*. ([excerpt](https://docs.farmingtoncanal.info/Harte_Connecticut_Canals_1938pps28-35.pdf))
- Harte, C. R. 1933. "[Some Engineering Features of the Old Northampton Canal](https://docs.farmingtoncanal.info/Harte_Northampton_Canal_1933.pdf)."
- Gay, J. 1929. *Farmington Papers*. ([excerpt](https://docs.farmingtoncanal.info/Farmington_Papers-Canal.pdf))
- Farnam, H. W. 1889. *[Henry Farnam](https://docs.farmingtoncanal.info/henryfarnam01farn.pdf)*.
- *[An Account of the Farmington Canal Company: Of the Hampshire and Hampden Canal Company; and of the New Haven and Northampton Company, Till the Suspension of Its Canals in 1847](https://www.google.com/books/edition/An_Account_of_the_Farmington_Canal_Compa/ILFLAAAAYAAJ)*. 1850.
